Participation Probability Analysis (2026)

Using data from the Williams Institute (UCLA) and NCAA Testimony, we can calculate the statistical likelihood of encountering a transgender athlete in women's sports.

The Formula:
P(Issue) = T * G * S
Where:
  • T (Transgender ID): 3.3% of youth population
  • G (Gender Alignment): ~33% (Trans girls within the trans population)
  • S (Sports Participation): ~12% (Active participation rate)

Key Findings

  • Structural Exclusion: As of 2026, 27 states have active bans, further depressing these numbers.
  • Elite Rarity: At the collegiate level, the presence of transgender women is so statistically rare it fails to register as a significant variable in general competition outcomes.
  • The "X" Variable: Your variable X (transgender girls playing sports) represents approximately 0.13% of the total student population.
