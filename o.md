* culmination
* closure
* perfection as endpoint

### **Plus Ultra**

* Literally: *“more beyond”* or *“further beyond.”*
* Historically adopted as the imperial motto of Spain (under Charles V) to reject the ancient limit.

Meaning:

* exploration
* expansion
* continuation beyond limits

So **Plus Ultra** represents:

* open horizons
* progress
* ongoing transcendence
